Namaste! Main aapka computer science teacher hoon. Aaj hum ek bahut hi important concept seekhenge: Primary Key (Database management mein).

Bilkul aasan bhasha mein samajhte hain!

Primary Key Kya Hoti Hai?

Sochiye ki aapke college mein hazaron students hain. Har student ka naam alag-alag ho sakta hai, lekin kai students ka naam "Rahul" ya "Priya" bhi ho sakta hai. Ab agar aapko kisi ek specific "Rahul" ke baare mein pata karna ho, toh aap confuse ho jaenge ki hum kis Rahul ki baat kar rahe hain.

Isi problem ko solve karne ke liye hum har student ko ek unique ID ya Roll Number dete hain, jo ki duniya ke kisi aur student ka nahi hota. Bas, database ki duniya mein usi unique ID ko hum Primary Key kehte hain!

Primary Key ki 3 Main Baatein:

Unique (Alag hoti hai): Isme koi bhi value repeat nahi ho sakti. Jaise agar ek student ka Roll Number 101 hai, toh kisi aur ka 101 nahi ho sakta.

Not Null (Khali nahi ho sakti): Primary Key wala column kabhi bhi khali (blank) nahi chhora ja sakta. Har record ke paas apni ek Primary Key honi hi chahiye.

Pehchan (Identification): Iski madad se hum kisi bhi row (data) ko bina kisi confusion ke turant dhoond sakte hain.

Ek Chhota sa Example:

Maan lijiye aapke paas ek table hai jisme students ka data hai:

| Roll Number (Primary Key) | Student Name | Course |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 101 | Amit Sharma | B.Tech |
| 102 | Priya Singh | BCA |
| 103 | Rohit Verma | B.Sc |

Yahan Roll Number ek Primary Key hai, kyunki isse hum har student ki exact pehchan kar sakte hain, chahe unke naam same hi kyun na ho.

Kya aapko Primary Key ka yeh concept samajh aaya? Agar isse juda koi bhi sawal ho, toh aap mujhse bejhijhak pooch sakte hain!
